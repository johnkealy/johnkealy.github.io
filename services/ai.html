What This Looks Like in the Field

Agent patterns that fit nonprofit work

Concrete examples of what agents do for mission-driven organizations — assisting your staff, not replacing their judgment.

Client Intake & Triage

Conversational or form-based agents collect unstructured client needs — housing, food aid, benefits — and check initial program eligibility in seconds, freeing staff for the human parts of the work.

Fundraising & Donor Engagement

Agents handle donor logistics: thank-you note reminders, personalized campaign emails, and follow-up management — so no donor slips through the cracks while your team focuses on relationships.

Volunteer & Community Coordination

Automated workflows match volunteers to open shifts, send reminders, log hours, and handle basic inquiries — the reliable drudgery every volunteer coordinator deserves to offload.

Grant Support

Agents scan funder databases, match programs to grants, and assist grant writers — cutting the boilerplate while your team keeps the judgment and the voice.

Coaching & Field Support

Digital coaching agents deliver localized, real-time guidance to remote community members or program participants — extending your program's reach beyond staffed hours and locations.

Your data, your models, your rules

What the industry calls "AI sovereignty" simply means the capability lives where you decide it lives. Run it on your servers, point it at your content, and audit what it does — instead of feeding your donors' data, patient questions, or student records into someone else's cloud and hoping their terms of service hold up.

  • Self-hosted deployments — your data never leaves your control
  • Open-source components — no vendor lock-in, no surprise licensing
  • Transparent outputs — every AI answer traceable to source content

Our stance on the AI hype cycle

Most AI content today is noise. We'd rather show you a working prototype on your data than a slide deck about transformation. If AI can't clearly save your team time or serve your audience better, we'll tell you that too.
