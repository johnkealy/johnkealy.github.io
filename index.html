Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why would we want a custom AI agent for our nonprofit?

A custom AI agent can answer donor inquiries, handle routine questions, and surface the right information for your communities (continuously, in your organization's voice) while your team handles the work that needs a human.

Why should we migrate to Drupal 11?

Drupal 11 is the most secure, full-featured version of Drupal. As Drupal 10 reaches end of security support on December 9, 2026. Our team has led many major Drupal upgrades, most recently the main UCSF website and around 20 custom sites from Drupal 10 to 11.

Why does our WordPress site need regular maintenance?

WordPress powers a huge share of the web, which makes it a constant target. The pace of core security releases has increased sharply in recent years. We keep core, plugins, and themes current, test every update in development and staging (with your review of staging before anything is deployed) and monitor for the vulnerabilities that actually get exploited.

What's included in retainer-based support?

Security updates and monitoring, performance optimization, quarterly accessibility audits, and dedicated hours for fixes and small enhancements — with transparent monthly reporting.

Can you support our existing Drupal or WordPress site?

Yes! We specialize in taking over existing sites and improving their security, performance, and compliance without a rebuild.
